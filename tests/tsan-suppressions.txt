# The ring reader (plugin/src/RingReader.h, readWith) copies a live ring's slots while the audio
# thread may be overwriting them (LiveSource::write), then decides which copied frames it keeps:
# the head R1 and the storage epoch read after an acquire fence (core::RingRead's protocol, a
# seqlock). TSan can't see that validation and reports the slot read in ringToBuffer against the
# write. The race is the design; every frame the reader keeps is checked (carry-over row 403).

# Orderly pause (the director, 2026-11-09)

The director asked to pause all M3 work in an orderly way, to resume later. Every lead:

1. **Starts nothing new:** no new task, reviewer, fix wave or final review.
2. **Lets work already in flight reach a clean point:** an implementer already running finishes its task and commits; a review already running finishes. A clean review merges into your branch as usual. If something can't finish in about 20 minutes, have it commit its state to its task branch as `wip: <what is left>`. Never discard work to get clean.
3. **Stops every background process you or your subagents started** (TaskStop, or the PIDs you started; never kill by name or pattern). Leave no `--watch` running.
4. **Leaves no uncommitted work anywhere.** Check `git status` in your worktree and every task worktree you made.
5. **Writes the resume point:**
   - in your own ledger: `PAUSED 2026-11-09. RESUME POINT: <done; next task; task branches and worktrees not yet merged, with heads; rulings pending; anything a fresh lead must know>`;
   - and one line in `docs/m3-progress/program-ledger.md`: `<sub-project>: PAUSED 2026-11-09 at <head>; next <task>`.
6. **Pushes your branch** if it has commits not on `origin` (a backup; an open draft PR gets the push, and you open no new PR).
7. **Reports** in at most 15 lines: head, what's done, the resume point, and anything for the director.

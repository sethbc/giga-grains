# M3 Color effects (drive, bit-crush, a scale-tuned resonator) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Each layer gains Drive (eight sst-waveshapers shapes, 2× oversampled, level-matched), Crush and Downsample after its buses; the instance gains an eight-band resonator bank tuned to its scale, after the layers' sum and before the delay. With every new parameter at its default nothing moves.

**Architecture:** Three in-house units in `core/src/fx/`: `Drive` (the only translation unit that includes sst-waveshapers; an in-house port of `HalfRateFilter` for the 2× path), `Crush` (bits and a sample-and-hold), and `Resonator` (16 TPT band-passes), plus a pure pitch module (`ResonatorPitch`) that turns the root, Chord, Follow and the held notes into eight pitches. The `Processor` runs Drive then Crush in `renderLayerPiece` between the bus sum and the diffuser, and the bank in `render`'s serial tail between the layers' sum and the delay. Every stage is gated by its ramp: at 0 it doesn't run and touches nothing. Sub-project 4's tuning table and sub-project 2's channel rule are reached through three one-line seams that their merges replace.

**Tech Stack:** C++23, sst-waveshapers `dd12f31` (GPL-3.0-or-later, header-only), sst-filters `87a171b` (`HalfRateFilter`, the reference only), sst-basic-blocks (`fasttan`, SIMD setup), Catch2, `gg-render` goldens, `gg-bench --chain`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-m3-color-design.md` (C1 to C15; parent `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-m3-program-design.md`). File:line references are at `6f4ff30` (this branch after merging `m3-voice` at `b8dd6ac`), and confirmed at `8c35af2` (`origin/main` merged: sub-project 1's final state, `CLAUDE.md` renamed `AGENTS.md`, `scripts/check-at-rest.sh` and its baseline); the merge moved no line this plan names.

### Task 4: Crush

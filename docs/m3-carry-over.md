**The policy is the M2 file's, which is the director's of 2026-10-30:** deferred items are handled as
superpowers' subagent-driven development handles them. A reviewer's minor is fixed or deferred at the
controller's judgment; deferred minors and items blocked outside our control (upstream, the
director's ears, a later feature) are recorded here as a plain log. There is no count rule and no
"touch it, close it" rule. A row stays until the thing it names is done, and nothing is filed
upstream.

Each moved row keeps the number it had in the M2 file (which kept M1's numbers), with its text,
source and "When" as they stood at the split, and a pointer back. Rows 184 and 404, which M3's
Voice scheduler was to close, are marked **Closed** with the commits that closed them. A row added
from now on takes the next free number from 406 up (M2's highest is 405), so no number names two
rows. A closed row stays in place, marked **Closed** in the "When" column with the commits that
closed it.
